## Hypothesis

The frozen hypothesis was that F's bounded deterministic discovery seam would produce a useful paired selection gain over A without regression. Its preregistered promotion condition failed: A1/2, F1/2, with the same failed case and no paired gain. Both lacked enough returned evidence for two required supported selections. This identifies inadequate task support in the packets; it does not establish whether sufficient evidence exists elsewhere, which retrieval mechanism would recover it, or whether additional reasoning would help.

## Smallest experiment

No new experiment proposed. The already completed mini supplied the required falsifier: exactly four native sessions and two offline judge batches, with initial and support assessments locked before resource unmasking. All four source-support judgments passed, but only one task per arm completed successfully. Honest limitations did not satisfy the other task. Close on these results without retuning, replacement, reruns, or expanding the benchmark to seek a positive outcome.

## Roles and cost reason

Retain one strong runtime planner/retriever/integrator with deterministic Aptuni execution; evaluation remains offline. No runtime specialist has demonstrated access to useful permitted information unavailable to A, or a sufficient-evidence reasoning failure requiring specialization. Reprocessing insufficient packets adds no documented remedy, and no evidence shows a cheaper deterministic seam is inadequate to a newly identified remedy. B matched A on one component at 2.55× tokens and 2.21× journey time. C/D remain unmeasured, not generally rejected; neither has earned further calls. Its measured journey was 18.38% faster, but timing confounds and the shared failure prevent an efficiency-based promotion. The mini's 43,921 judge tokens are separate research overhead.

## Conditional decision

Apply the frozen failure branch now: no F production or research-finalist promotion; A remains the measured reference. This closes the candidate comparison without claiming the underlying discovery problem is solved or impossible. Reopening requires a genuinely new falsifiable bottleneck, a bounded development result on separate material, and only then a fresh independently frozen evaluation under unchanged permissions. That is a reopening condition, not an additional experiment commissioned here. These cases cannot become hidden tuning followed by claimed unseen validation.
