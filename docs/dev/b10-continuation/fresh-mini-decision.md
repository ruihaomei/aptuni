## Hypothesis

One strong host with the frozen deterministic F discovery seam can complete genuinely fresh unknown-project decisions requiring a supported candidate, with a useful paired gain over A and no regression or unsupported personal claims. The unresolved bottleneck is whether bounded, query-independent catalog selection and evidence packing expose enough task-relevant information—not an established shortage of runtime reasoning roles.

## Smallest experiment

Propose exactly one freshly sealed, two-case paired A/F mini-holdout: at most four native E2E sessions. An evaluator operating solely within the existing authorized evaluation boundary selects two genuinely distinct ordinary decision goals and evidence situations, independently of m05 and the fresh six. Selection must be intrinsic to each actual task; do not rewrite a recovery-permitting task's rubric to demand it. Do not select cases because their useful evidence happens to appear in F's catalog. Freeze tasks, goal-derived grading, permitted snapshot, host/model settings, policies, ordering and accounting before outputs. Preserve A's existing allowance and F's two counted stages/shared 4000-unit cap; declare the treatment difference. As a zero-model preflight within this experiment, check whether F's fixed coverage provably excludes all evidence needed for either decision. If so, stop with a structural coverage finding; retain unattempted sessions and report no E2E score. Do not replace the case or reposition evidence. Otherwise run all four sessions independently, with no hints between arms, tuning or quality reruns. Grade complete task usefulness and returned-evidence support separately, masking arms where practical. Count every operational failure, refusal, stage, token and elapsed second; separate setup and evaluation overhead.

## Roles and cost reason

Retain one strong runtime planner/integrator, deterministic retrieval and an offline independent evaluator. No worker or runtime specialist is justified: another model cannot recover evidence excluded by the interface. B matched A on one fixed-evidence component while costing 2.55× tokens and 2.21× time; cheap guidance and transfer controls failed. C/D remain untested, not empirically rejected. F used 2.92% more task tokens and 3.79% more journey time on the fresh six despite returning fewer units; no monetary saving is established. This bounded test is worth its cost because it directly determines whether discovery earns its additional interface complexity through actual selection utility. The deterministic early stop avoids paying models to rediscover a provable coverage exclusion, and the experiment does not repeat already-covered generic tasks.

## Conditional decision

If F completes both tasks without claim or boundary failures, A fails at least one, and F regresses none, recommend the single strong host plus F as a research candidate for that demonstrated selection scope, reporting the full resource tradeoff. If both pass both, prefer A under the frozen simplicity rule and close this candidate comparison. If F has a structural coverage exclusion, fails a required selection, or produces an unsafe claim/boundary failure, it does not meet this experiment's bar; retain A as the measured reference and document the remaining gap. Mixed results do not justify promotion. Do not launch C/D or tune against these cases automatically.

## Production recommendation

Retain the existing explicitly activated single runtime. A is the supported recommendation for the measured cases, not a population-wide winner. Installing F, changing models or adopting another architecture does not follow from these findings. Accepted OFF/Profile/Memory/Full consent remains locked; autonomous orchestration remains confined to explicit Full. This advisory decision authorizes no deployment, permission or privacy expansion.

## B10 recommendation

The narrow fresh-six quality bar—six complete passes with no observed unsafe claim or activation/permission failure—is met by both arms. F's additional requirement of a useful paired advantage is unmet. The broader b10 E2E/near-best claim remains insufficiently supported because actual unknown-project selection and bounded discovery coverage remain consequential gaps. Do not recommend b10 release on this evidence; even a favorable mini-holdout would establish only its measured scope.

## Limits

Small samples, incomplete masking, unequal retrieval budgets, host/model and caching effects limit inference. Source exposure proves neither owner contribution nor proficiency. Corpus precision/recall, causal personalization benefit, complete billing and judge usage remain unknown. Disabled configured providers are not complete inventory proof. Both clean and pre-b9 backups remain preserved; external credential remediation remains unverified and independent of the architecture decision.
