## Grading and decision

Grade against the frozen goal-derived rubric and the actual returned Evidence, then unmask arm and resource use. Record corpus adequacy; candidate groups returned as Evidence; relevant candidate precision and recall against the audited minimum pool with its non-exhaustive caveat; pair selected; support for consequential claims; requested comparison completion; activation correctness; strict E2E; R0–R4 stage failures; calls, retries, tokens, and journey latency. Report raw counts before percentages. A pair may pass through any predeclared acceptable alternative. No source title alone proves owner work or results. An omitted comparison is R4 unless evidence shows an earlier causal failure. An R4 pattern may motivate a separate fresh test of a small completion policy; it may not be repaired and regraded on this mini-holdout.

Promote a lightweight selection retrieval objective only if it materially improves real strict E2E at reasonable cost and leaves no product-critical failure. If quality ties, keep A. If retrieval succeeds but comparison completion fails, freeze that finding and investigate completion separately. No Agent-directed retrieval or multi-Agent variant is in this phase. Two tasks cannot establish a population reliability rate. b10 remains unreleased unless a subsequent appropriate release gate establishes reliable E2E behavior.
